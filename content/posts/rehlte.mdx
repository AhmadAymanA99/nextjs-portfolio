## The problem

Bisan Travel needed a hotel-booking app that feels native to travellers in both English and Arabic. That means a full right-to-left layout, not a translated copy, and card payments that pass 3-D Secure bank checks without dropping users out of the app halfway through a booking.

## What I did

I own the payment, booking-management and account modules:

• **Payments:** card payments with 3-D Secure through HyperPay, including the bank-verification step and its success, failure and cancel paths
• **Accounts:** OTP-based phone verification and Google Sign-In
• **Bookings:** booking and trip management, from confirmation to upcoming and past trips
• **Bilingual UI:** full English/Arabic support with RTL layouts
• **Stability:** Android performance and stability fixes, working closely with the backend team on QA issues

## The result

The app is in QA ahead of its public launch on the App Store and Google Play.
