## Introduction:
Rehlte is a bilingual (English/Arabic RTL) hotel-booking mobile app for iOS and Android, with secure in-app payments, trip management, and user accounts. It is currently in QA ahead of launch.

### My Contribution:
As part of the Bisan Travel team, I own the payment, booking-management, and account modules:
• Card payments with 3-D Secure through HyperPay
• OTP-based verification and Google Sign-In
• Booking and trip management
• Full English/Arabic RTL support
• Android performance and stability improvements

### Technologies:
React Native, Expo (Expo Router), TypeScript, Zustand, TanStack Query, Reanimated
