## The problem

Grip gives restaurants and cafés a digital menu: customers scan a QR code at the table and browse dishes, prices and offers on their own phone, in Arabic or English. The menu had to load fast on mobile data and be easy for each venue to keep up to date.

## What I did

As part of the Vidills team I built front-end features:

• Menu browsing: categories, item pages and grid/list views
• Reusable React components and state management with Redux Toolkit
• Integration with REST and GraphQL back-ends
• Performance work for faster loads on phones

## The result

The product launched and was in use by venues while it was online.
