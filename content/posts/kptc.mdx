## The problem

Kuwait Public Transport Company (KPTC) runs Kuwait's public bus network. It needed two things: a bilingual public website where riders find routes and services, apply for jobs, follow tenders and pay online, and a back office where staff manage all of that content and the requests coming in, without asking developers to change pages.

## What I did

**Admin dashboard** (React, Redux Toolkit, TanStack Query). I was the main developer, with 250+ commits:

• Content management for every page of the public site: home, about, services, routes, tenders, jobs, media center, clients, legal pages
• Operations modules: bus leasing and leasing orders, service subscriptions, card validation, receiving locations, offices and areas
• Payments: payment methods and payment links
• Users and permission groups, reports with charts, a calendar, and contact messages
• A modular structure, so new modules plug in without touching existing ones

**Public website** (Next.js, English/Arabic with full RTL). I built the rider-facing account features:

• Sign-up, sign-in, sign-out and profile, wired to the API
• Service subscriptions, with responsive, RTL-aware subscription cards
• A digital ID card with a QR code generated per rider
• Service cards, sliders, and the privacy, terms and contact pages

## The result

KPTC's staff manage the public site and its service requests from one dashboard, in English and Arabic.
