## The problem

Awafi is a Saudi platform for remote psychological care (psychiatry, cognitive behavioural therapy and family medicine) supervised by Dr. Khalid Bin Hamad Al-Jaber. Patients assess the severity of their symptoms, book sessions and follow treatment programs online. The product had to be Arabic-first, easy to use for people in distress, and careful with highly sensitive health data. It is accredited by the Saudi Ministry of Health.

## What I did

As part of the Softera team I built and supported core patient-facing modules:

• Appointment booking and the patient workflows around it
• Validated healthcare forms and assessments
• Full Arabic/English support with RTL layouts and Arabic-digit formatting
• Secure handling of sensitive health data in the front end
• Production releases, analytics monitoring and bug fixing

## The result

Awafi Clinic is live, and patients use it to book and attend remote sessions in Arabic or English.
