## The problem

Al Fagala sells kitchen, bathroom and building fixtures through 10 branches in four Egyptian cities. It wanted its full catalogue online for customers nationwide, with the payment options its customers already use in store: installment plans, cash on delivery and price quotes. It also launched Al Fagala Express, delivery in under 30 minutes in October City and Sheikh Zayed.

## What I did

• Built storefront pages for the catalogue: categories, brands, search and product pages
• Arabic-first RTL layout that works on phones as well as desktop
• SEO for product and category pages

## The result

The store is live at alfagala.com and serves customers across Egypt.
