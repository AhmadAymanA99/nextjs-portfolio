## The problem

Procoor builds software for the construction industry. **Procoor PM** coordinates construction projects (document approvals, submittals, contracts), and **Procoor FM** runs buildings after handover (maintenance schedules, service requests, SLAs). Both are data-heavy dashboards used daily by large teams, so slow screens and front-end bugs directly cost customers time.

## What I did

I developed and maintained front-end features across both products:

• Built and improved dashboard and workflow screens, wired to the platform's APIs
• Took ownership of critical front-end bugs and resolved 97% of them, cutting downtime for customers
• Improved performance and usability of the busiest screens
• Helped move the team to Agile workflows (sprints, smaller releases)

## The result

The two apps serve **600K+ users**, and engagement rose by **95%** over my time on the team. The move to Agile cut delivery cycle time by **50%** and raised sprint completion rates by **80%**.
